LANTERN THIEF — The Silent Gallery

A moonlit museum. Three relics. Four watchmen. One quiet exit.

Move WASD or arrow keys  ·  Sneak hold Shift (slow, quiet, harder to spot)  ·  Throw a pebble click the floor (3 pebbles — guards investigate the noise)  ·  Pause P  ·  Restart R  ·  Mute M
Stay out of the yellow cones. Crates and display cases block a guard's sight; dark floor patches hide you. Steal all three relics, then slip out the green exit.
